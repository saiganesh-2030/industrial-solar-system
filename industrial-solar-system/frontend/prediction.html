Normalised Yield Methodology: The ML model outputs AC power in kW based on a multi-megawatt utility plant. To apply this to factory rooftop or ground-mount systems, the model generation is divided by the reference plant's observed peak to obtain a unitless yield fraction (0.0 to 1.0), which is then multiplied by the factory's target capacity in kW.

Faculty Verification Note on Module Temperature: Weather forecast APIs only provide ambient air temperature. PV panels heat significantly under solar absorption.
